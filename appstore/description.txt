RunCat for Pebble

A playful watchface where the official RunCat runs faster as your heart rate rises.

Features:
- Animated RunCat running in place
- Animation speed changes with your heart rate
- Current time and date
- Current heart rate display
- Works when heart rate data is unavailable

The cat keeps running at a default speed when heart rate data cannot be accessed.

Designed primarily for Pebble Time 2 and compatible with supported rectangular Pebble watches.
